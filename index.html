How to use

A gentle three-step ritual.

A quiet few minutes after bath time or before sleep.

Pour

Take a little oil in your palm and warm it between your hands.

Massage

Apply all over the body using slow, gentle and rhythmic strokes.

Tummy circles

Move in a clockwise direction on the tummy, at your baby's pace.

CautionFor external use only. Avoid contact with eyes; rinse immediately if contact occurs. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Patch test recommended before use. Keep out of reach of children.
StorageStore below 25°C. Do not freeze. Protect from direct sunlight. Plant-based oils can change colour over time; this does not affect the product.
